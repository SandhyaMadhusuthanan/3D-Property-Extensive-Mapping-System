# Features

## 1. Role-Based Login
- Two simulated roles: **Government Official** and **Property Owner / Citizen**.
- Demo credentials provided directly on screen (no real account checking — local simulation only).

## 2. Government Dashboard
- City-wide snapshot: total area (e.g., 125 acres), total plots (320), total buildings (24), and portfolio value (₹256 Cr+).
- Quick-access panel of sample households for demo purposes (2, 3, 4, and 6-resident households).

## 3. Interactive Overview Map
- **Map / Satellite** toggle view.
- Category filters: **All, Lands, Apartments, Shops, Complexes**.
- Elevated transit corridor overlay — click the line for project details affecting nearby land.
- Click any plot or building to drill into its full record.

## 4. Property Categories
- **New Lands** — plotted layouts and individual land parcels.
- **Joint Ownership** — group-purchased land with shared ULPIN.
- **Apartments** — multi-floor residential buildings.
- **Individual Houses** — standalone residential units.
- **Shops** — individual commercial units.
- **Complexes** — mixed-use shop and office buildings.

## 5. Joint Ownership Module
- Single shared ULPIN used by every member.
- "All Members Aadhaar-Linked" verification badge.
- Land details: location, total extent, total purchase value, ownership type.
- **Share Contribution by Member** — each member's undivided percentage share.
- **Ownership History** — registration events and share transfers over time.
- **Former Owners** — members who sold their full share, Aadhaar unlinked, contact retained for record purposes only.

## 6. Land Layout & Plot View
- All plots in a layout shown with status tags: **Sold**, **Yours**, available.
- Government project proximity warnings (🚧/🚇 icons) where an upcoming project may affect land rate.
- Plot-level details: length, width, facing direction, status.
- "Find this plot on site" + "Get directions" actions.

## 7. Land Value & 10-Year Projection
- Base guideline value per cent for the street.
- Line-by-line breakdown of how nearby government amenities each add a measurable premium.
- Year-by-year projected value chart for the next 10 years.
- Government project milestones priced into the projection curve.
- Explicit disclaimer that projections are indicative, not a valuation certificate.

## 8. Vertical Rights Snapshot
- Shows every rights layer of a single plot — from below the surface to air rights above — reinforcing the core 3D-ULPIN concept even for a single plot.

## 9. Building 3D Model & Floor-by-Floor Breakdown
- **View 3D Model** — rotatable, zoomable exterior building concept (front, rear, left, right, roof elevations), with Auto 360° rotation.
- Floor count, total units, plot area, and built-up area shown per building.
- **Vertical Ownership Layers** — click any layer (floor, unit, parking, air rights) to see its own sub-parcel ULPIN.
- Unit types color-coded: individually titled unit, vacant unit, entrance/ground common, terrace/common area, basement parking, and unbuilt air rights.

## 10. Parking as an Independent Record
- Every parking bay carries its own sub-parcel ULPIN.
- Bay types: **Allotted to a unit, Visitor bay, EV charging bay, Unallotted**.
- Shops get their own allotted surface parking bays outside their frontage.

## 11. Household / Unit Detail View
- Household summary: occupancy type, number of residents, member names/ages/relations.
- Allotted parking bay shown against the unit.
- Clear disclaimer that household data is illustrative/demo-only, not sourced from a live civil registry.

## 12. Find My Plot (Navigation Tool)
- Helps a buyer standing at a layout with many similar-looking plots identify their exact plot.
- Shows distance to plot and estimated walk time.
- "Start outdoor navigation" for real GPS-guided directions.
- Honest limitation noted: exact-peg accuracy requires surveyed corner points or a physical marker.

## 13. Shop / Complex Records
- Shop area, frontage, usage type, and ownership.
- Linked allotted parking bays with their own sub-parcel ULPINs.

## 14. Nearby Government Amenities Panel
- Lists government amenities near a given plot and explains how each contributes to the land's guideline-value premium.
